2. Scope of Service & Local Execution

The Software runs locally on the user's personal Windows computer. Its functionalities include:

  • Assisting in scientific literature synthesis and educational claim verification.
  • Generating video production scripts, storyboards, and visual assets.
  • Synthesizing Vietnamese audio narration using local neural models (VieNeu-TTS).
