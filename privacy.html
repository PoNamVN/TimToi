Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Last Updated: October 26, 2026 • Local-First Desktop Application

Core Privacy Principle: Tim Toi Studio is a local Windows desktop application, NOT a software-as-a-service (SaaS) or cloud web service. All authentication credentials, access tokens, production scripts, audio voiceovers, and rendered video files reside strictly on your local computer. We do not operate any external user tracking server or remote credential database.

1. Who We Are

Tim Toi Studio is an independent educational video creation tool developed by Po Nam ("Developer"), based in Vietnam. This Privacy Policy explains how our software interacts with external platforms (specifically TikTok and YouTube) and handles user data during video creation and publishing.

2. TikTok OAuth 2.0 Authorization & Data Requested

To enable video publishing capabilities directly to TikTok, Tim Toi Studio implements the official TikTok Content Posting API using an OAuth 2.0 PKCE (Proof Key for Code Exchange) flow designed for native desktop applications.

A. Requested Scopes

  • user.info.basic: Used exclusively to retrieve your public TikTok display name, avatar URL, and open_id. This information is displayed in the local desktop review console to help the creator confirm which TikTok profile is currently linked.
  • video.upload: Used to upload the finalized MP4 video file to the creator's TikTok Inbox / Drafts folder. Uploading to Inbox does not make content public; it allows the creator to inspect and finalize publishing within the official TikTok mobile app.
  • video.publish: (Only when approved by platform review and enabled by the creator): Used to publish videos directly as scheduled or public posts, accompanied by the mandatory is_aigc: true compliance tag.

B. What We NEVER Access

Tim Toi Studio does not request, collect, or store:

  • Your TikTok account password or login credentials.
  • Your direct messages, chat logs, or comments.
  • Your following/followers list or personal contacts.
  • Your viewing history, search history, or browsing habits.
  • Payment, credit card, or financial details.

3. Local Storage of Authentication Tokens

When you grant authorization through the official TikTok login page:

  • The authorization code is exchanged directly between your local PC and https://open.tiktokapis.com over TLS/HTTPS.
  • The returned access_token, refresh_token, and expiration timestamp are saved locally in the project secrets directory: secrets/tiktok_token.json.
  • No External Server Transfer: These tokens are NEVER transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any remote web server, cloud backend, or third party.
  • Tokens are utilized solely by your local workstation to execute authorized API requests on your command.

4. Video Production & Content Data Handling

All stages of video production occur on your local machine:

  • Audio Narration: Voice synthesis is performed locally on-device using the VieNeu-TTS neural model. No spoken audio or voice samples are uploaded to external speech servers.
  • Video Rendering: Video compositing and FFmpeg rendering are completed locally on your CPU/GPU.
  • Transmission Gate: No video file or metadata is uploaded to TikTok or YouTube until you explicitly trigger publishing via the interactive Telegram bot or local CLI, with verified cryptographic SHA-256 hash matching.

5. Third-Party Services Used

The Software connects directly to the following official third-party APIs:

  • TikTok Content Posting API (ByteDance Ltd.): For creator authentication, querying creator account capability, and uploading videos to Inbox or Direct Post. Governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Data API v3 (Google LLC): For YouTube Shorts upload and publishing verification. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): This public static documentation website loads typography stylesheets from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When visiting these pages, your web browser requests font files directly from Google, which may log standard HTTP request metadata in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API: Utilized by the local research and scripting agents for scientific literature summarization. No personal account credentials or private tokens are sent to Gemini.
  • Telegram Bot API: Utilized exclusively as a private human-in-the-loop review interface between the local production daemon and the authorized creator chat ID.

6. Data Retention and How to Delete Your Data

Because all data is stored locally on your machine, you have complete control over data retention and deletion at all times:

A. Immediate Local Token Deletion

You can immediately purge all stored tokens, credentials, and cached creator profile data by deleting the token files from your local workstation:

  • Delete secrets/tiktok_token.json to remove all stored TikTok session keys.
  • Delete secrets/youtube_token.json to remove all stored YouTube Google session keys.

B. Revoking Access Directly via TikTok

You can revoke Tim Toi Studio's authorization at any moment directly from within your TikTok app:

  1. Open the TikTok mobile application.
  2. Navigate to your Profile → tap the menu icon (☰) → select Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap Security (or Security and permissions) → tap Manage app permissions.
  4. Locate Tim Toi Studio and tap Remove access.

Once revoked, all previously issued access tokens will become immediately invalid.

C. Requesting Deletion Assistance

Because we do not operate a remote user database, we do not store your data on external servers. However, if you have any questions or require confirmation regarding data deletion, you may email us directly at phanlephuongnam4@gmail.com and we will assist you within 48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Tim Toi Studio is a developer and creator production workstation tool intended for adults and teenage science creators of legal age to hold TikTok and YouTube developer accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
